What Is It?

Built for Real Schools

Proton School Manager is a full School ERP — not a basic app. It handles every administrative function a school needs, from student admission to annual results, stored securely on your own computer with zero cloud dependency.

Works fully offline. Install on one PC or across your entire school via LAN. Multi-branch and multi-campus support included.
